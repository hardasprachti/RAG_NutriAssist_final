According to official guidance from the World Health Organization (WHO) and the ICMR-National Institute of Nutrition (2024 update) , healthy adults (ages 19–70) require:

Daily Recommended RDA 600 – 800 IU (15 – 20 mcg / day)
Tolerable Upper Limit (UL) 4,000 IU / day FDA / EFSA safety cap

☀️ Sunlight synthesis: 15–25 minutes of midday sun exposure (face, arms, and hands without sunscreen) generates roughly 1,000–2,000 IU in fair-to-medium skin tones, regulated naturally without risk of toxicity.

🐟 Dietary sources: Wild salmon (400–600 IU per 100g), fortified plant milks (100–120 IU per cup), and egg yolks (40 IU each) .

What are the best bioavailable food sources for Calcium if someone is strictly lactose intolerant?
🫐
EFSA & WHO Validated Data

For lactose-intolerant individuals, bioavailable non-dairy calcium sources should factor in oxalate and phytate inhibition ratios 1:

  • Calcium-set Tofu: 350–400 mg per 100g (Absorption ~31%, identical to dairy milk) 2
  • Bok Choy & Broccoli: High bioavailability (~50–60% fractional absorption due to low oxalates).
  • Fortified Plant Milks: Typically 300 mg per cup (must shake well as tricalcium phosphate settles).
